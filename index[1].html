How this works

  • Frontend collects your progress and sends it to the Python backend.
  • FastAPI validates the inputs and runs the academic risk and study-plan algorithm.
  • The backend returns JSON results; JavaScript updates the dashboard.
  • “Save in this browser” stores your entries in localStorage on this device/browser only.

Privacy note: do not enter passwords, government IDs, or sensitive personal information. Browser storage is not a cloud database.
